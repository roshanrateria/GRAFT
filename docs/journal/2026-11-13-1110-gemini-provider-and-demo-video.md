## Entry: 2026-11-13 Gemini provider fix and demo video production
- Kiro surface used: IDE (agent session)
- Feature(s) used: vibe | steering | PBT | hook | spec
- What I asked / how I structured it: Two goals: (1) make the live Doctor pipeline reliable on the Gemini OpenAI-compatible endpoint (the stream was being truncated mid-JSON, killing the build stage), and (2) produce the Kiroween demo video end to end — presentation card deck, Playwright recorder, TTS narration, ffmpeg assembler, final take. I verified the pipeline with smoke wishes first, fixed the provider, then recorded a dry run to shake out recording bugs before the final take.
- What worked:
  - Diagnosing the truncation from `[llm]` server logs ("stream finished: 6 content chunks" for a ~3 KB JSON) and confirming non-streamed requests were reliable. Fix in `packages/doctor/src/providers/llm.ts`: for Gemini base URLs send `reasoning_effort: 'low'` and `max_tokens >= 16384` (thinking spends the token budget), and auto-retry any streamed response whose JSON is unparseable as a non-streamed request. NVIDIA path untouched (`chat_template_kwargs` stays NVIDIA-only). Smoke wish went from broken to wish → consent-request in ~4.9 s.
  - Recording the whole demo as one Playwright session with per-scene wall-clock times logged to `scenes.json`, then cutting app scenes from the continuous recording by offsets — frame-accurate without N browser recordings.
  - Dry run catching real bugs before the final take: app rendered light theme (fixed with `colorScheme: 'dark'` — the app theme is `prefers-color-scheme`-driven), and caption/fade overlays were wiped by every `page.goto` (overlays now re-installed defensively on every navigation).
  - ChartPanel now polls `/api/ledger/history` every 5 s. On a fresh ledger the Verify button used to stay disabled forever (empty list from the mount-time fetch); live polling also makes The Chart update during the demo. Loading spinner kept for the first fetch only so the initial-state test stays green.
  - Narration fitting: the TTS voice runs slow, so the assembler speeds each clip to its scene length with `atempo`, capped at 1.4×.
- What failed or surprised me:
  - First final take died at the ledger scene: Verify chain disabled with a fresh ledger (the ChartPanel bug above). Everything re-recorded after the fix.
  - Gemini free-tier TTS quota is tight: 429s that need ~65 s spacing; the TTS script waits those out, skips a clip after 10 attempts, and the assembler tolerates missing clips (captions carry the beat).
  - First TTS attempt parsed `--out` as the voice name (`args[args.indexOf('--voice') + 1] ?? 'Kore'` with no `--voice` flag) — voice resolution had to be index-guarded.
  - The 3 CHI-01 failures in `chimera-linking.test.ts` remain (pre-existing, documented); I did not touch `ChimeraLinking.ts`.
